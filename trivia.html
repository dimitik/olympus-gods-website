Side quests

Trivia the oracle would actually enjoy.

Real classical facts, lightly overclocked. Use them at parties. Or at temples. The mountain does not fact-check your toast.

01 / Roster drama

Hestia gave up her seat.

The starting twelve was a lineup debate. Hestia, goddess of the hearth, once held an Olympian chair and stepped aside so Dionysus could join. Oldest roommate energy. She kept the fireplace; he brought the afterparty.

02 / Workplace wellness

Athena was a migraine with a spear.

She burst fully armored from Zeus’s head after he swallowed her mother Metis. Olympus still files the incident under “do not reproduce at home.” Wisdom, it turns out, is a splitting headache.

03 / Day-one exit

Hermes shipped a lyre before lunch.

On the first day of his life he invented the lyre, then traded it to Apollo. Startup exits have been downhill ever since. He also stole Apollo’s cattle, which is a bold onboarding plan.

04 / Soft launch

Aphrodite’s origin is an oceanic press event.

One tradition says she rose from sea-foam after a very messy family meeting between sky and sea. The official brochure calls it a “foam-based product launch.” The unofficial one just says “do not google Uranus.”

05 / Contractor of the year

Hephaestus got thrown off the mountain. Twice.

Depending on the poet, he was hurled from Olympus as an infant or after taking Hera’s side. He still came back and built everyone’s weapons. That is the most professional grudge in myth.

06 / Terms of service

Artemis negotiated her own DLC.

She asked Zeus for eternal maidenhood, a bow, a choir of nymphs, and a city. The original terms-of-service renegotiation — and she got it. Personal space, but make it divine policy.

07 / Skill issue

Ares, god of war, once lived in a jar.

Giants stuffed him in a bronze jar for thirteen months until Hermes sprung him. The god of war. In a jar. Please clap. Volume is not the same thing as a plan.

08 / Seasonal content

Winter is Demeter’s out-of-office.

When Persephone is in the underworld, Demeter stops the harvest. The custody agreement is the original content drop: six months of grain, six months of “the server is sad.”

09 / Civic pitch

Athens picked salad over spa water.

Poseidon and Athena competed to name the city. He offered a salt spring (or a horse, depending on the cut). She offered the olive tree. The judges chose snacks, oil, and wood. Poseidon is still in the group chat about it.

10 / Hardware

Zeus outsources his lightning.

The Cyclopes forged the thunderbolts. Even the king of the gods does not 3D-print his own hardware. Hephaestus handles the later firmware; the Cyclopes still get a credit in the end titles.

11 / Prestige TV

Dionysus invented going to the theater.

His festivals in Athens grew into tragedy and comedy. Every prestige show with a sad king and a chorus of opinions owes a grape a thank-you note. The god of wine also shipped drama as a service.

12 / Downstairs cousin

Hades is not on the mountain.

He is a brother of Zeus and Poseidon, but he clocks in below. That is why he is missing from the twelve. He still RSVPs “maybe” to reunions and brings excellent wine, which is awkward for Dionysus.
