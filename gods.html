The twelve who actually live upstairs.

Hades commutes from the basement and is not on this org chart. Everyone else is right here — glowing, floating, and slightly overqualified for weather.

King of the sky

Zeus

Cloud CEO, professional thunder enthusiast, and part-time shapeshifter. Married to Hera, which is a full-time diplomatic posting. Rules Olympus from a throne that doubles as a weather dashboard.

Abilities

A weather app with anger issues. Can throw a bolt farther than your group chat can throw shade. Also legally a swan if the vibe is right.

Queen of the gods

Hera

Patron of marriage, protector of the throne, and chief inspector of Zeus’s calendar. Travels with a peacock surveillance network that has never once lost a receipt.

Abilities

Unbreakable vows, side-eye that can curdle nectar, and the power to turn any wedding seating chart into a moral test.

Lord of the sea

Poseidon

Landlord of seventy-one percent of the planet. Invented the earthquake as a noise complaint and still thinks horses were a good idea. Brings a trident to every city-planning meeting.

Abilities

Instant tidal upgrade, trident-based home renovation, and the talent for making your cruise “experiential.”

Lady of the harvest

Demeter

Goddess of grain, seasons, and the original farm-to-table movement. When her daughter Persephone is away, she invents winter out of grief, spite, and snacks.

Abilities

Crop-yield miracle, seasonal climate policy, and bread so good it should require a hymn.

Wisdom and war-plans

Athena

Sprang from Zeus’s skull fully armored, which remains the mountain’s most extra birth story. Patron of crafts, cities, and winning the argument before it starts.

Abilities

Strategic overthink, owl-powered intel, and making your startup pitch sound like a siege plan.

Sun, song, and spoilers

Apollo

God of the sun, music, medicine, and prophecy. The golden boy who actually practiced. Runs Delphi like a luxury help desk that answers only in riddles.

Abilities

A playlist that heals, UV on demand, and customer support so cryptic it never gets a one-star review.

Moon and the wild

Artemis

Huntress of the night, protector of wild things and of not being bothered. Twin of Apollo, better aim, worse at small talk. Prefers forests to family dinners.

Abilities

Night-vision bow, instant camping permit, and a fifty-meter personal-space field.

God of the brawl

Ares

Patron of war and of starting fights in comment sections. Surprisingly easy to beat at strategy, extremely hard to beat at volume. Bronze is his entire personality.

Abilities

Battle-rage turbo, clank-based surround sound, and the power to turn a picnic into a tournament.

Love, looks, leverage

Aphrodite

Born of sea-foam, professional heart-thief, unofficial stylist of Olympus. Can start a war with a glance and end one with a slightly better outfit.

Abilities

Charm with a user agreement, dove-based PR, and making armor look like a date night.

Forge and firmware

Hephaestus

Divine blacksmith, inventor, and the only Olympian with a real job. Builds legendary hardware for people who will immediately misuse it. Keeps the volcano HVAC running.

Abilities

Instant legendary craft, magma climate control, and debugging thunderbolts Zeus keeps overclocking.

Messenger, thief, tour guide

Hermes

Fastest man in a winged hat. Invented the lyre on day one of being alive, then traded it. Also walks souls downstairs, which is a lot for one intern-god.

Abilities

Same-day delivery, identity remix, and reading your group chat before you hit send.

Wine, theater, release

Dionysus

The late addition who joined the starting twelve and never let anyone forget the glow-up. God of wine, theater, and letting go — usually in that order, loudly.

Abilities

Mood upgrade, vine-based party infrastructure, and turning a Tuesday into a festival with three notes of a hymn.

Olympus

The cloud layer with better columns. Firmware 12.0, still in open beta since the Bronze Age.

How to get here

Take the Cloud Elevator from Athens Terminal, transfer at Cumulus Hub, then hail a Pegasus (surge pricing during thunderstorms). Bring photo ID and a reasonably polite goat. Sandals off in the Lightning Lounge. Please do not feed the hydra — it is unionized.
